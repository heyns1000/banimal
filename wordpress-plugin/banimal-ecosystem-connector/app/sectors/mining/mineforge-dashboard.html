What is MineForge™?

MineForge™ is FAA’s blockchain-integrated AI validation suite for yield assurance in mining. Built to assist miners, auditors, and regulatory authorities, MineForge™ provides a tamper-proof digital layer over extraction operations. It validates the mineral grade, quantity, and purity in real-time, logs extraction proof via blockchain, and flags fraud risks using predictive AI.
